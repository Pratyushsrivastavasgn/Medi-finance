## Q&A PREPARATION

**Expected Questions & Answers:**

**Q: How do you ensure pricing accuracy?**
A: We aggregate data from multiple sources - hospital APIs, historical patient bills, insurance claim data, and real-time updates from hospital partners. Our AI model achieves 87% confidence through continuous learning.

**Q: What's your competitive moat?**
A: Network effects - more hospitals = better data = better predictions = more patients. Plus, multi-sided platform with hospitals, insurers, and NBFCs all locked in.

**Q: Customer acquisition cost?**
A: B2B hospital partnerships provide built-in patient base. CAC ≈ ₹200, primarily digital marketing. Payback in 3-4 transactions.

**Q: Regulatory concerns?**
A: We're a technology platform, not a healthcare provider. No IRDA license needed as we don't sell insurance. NBFC partnerships handle lending compliance.

**Q: How do you monetize free features?**
A: Freemium model - basic comparison free, advanced features (detailed reports, priority support) paid. Primary revenue from B2B and commissions.

**Q: What about patient privacy?**
A: HIPAA-compliant infrastructure, encryption at rest and in transit, no PII sold to third parties, GDPR ready for international expansion.
