# Revenue Model - MediFinance Platform

## Executive Summary

MediFinance operates a **multi-sided marketplace** connecting patients, hospitals, insurance companies, and financing partners. We generate revenue through B2B SaaS subscriptions, transaction-based commissions, and premium features while maintaining a freemium patient-facing platform.

**Target ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue):** ₹10 Cr by Year 2

**Primary Revenue Streams:**
1. B2B SaaS for Hospitals (60% of revenue)
2. Insurance Partner Commissions (25% of revenue)
3. NBFC/Bank Referral Fees (12% of revenue)
4. Premium Features & API Access (3% of revenue)

---

## Revenue Stream 2: Insurance Partner Commissions

### Commission Structure

#### Lead Generation Fee
- **₹200-500 per qualified lead** sent to insurance companies
- Qualified = user views policy recommendations + clicks to insurer site

#### Policy Sale Commission
- **2-5% of first-year premium** for policies sold through platform
- Example: ₹15,000 premium → ₹450-750 commission

#### Renewal Commission
- **1-2% of renewal premiums** (Years 2-5)
- Passive income from existing customer base

#### Data Licensing Fee
- **₹5L-10L per quarter** from insurers for anonymized insights
  - Hospital pricing trends
  - Patient behavior analytics
  - Claim pattern analysis

## Revenue Stream 3: NBFC/Bank Referral Fees

### Commission Structure

#### Zero-Cost EMI (BNPL)
- **1-2% of loan amount** from BNPL providers
- Example: ₹50,000 loan → ₹500-1,000 commission

#### Medical Loans
- **2-3% of loan amount** from NBFCs/banks
- Example: ₹2,00,000 loan → ₹4,000-6,000 commission

#### Embedded Finance (Future)
- **Co-branded credit products:** ₹500-1,000 per approved card

## Unit Economics

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

#### Patient CAC
- **Digital Marketing:** ₹150-200/user
- **Hospital Partnerships:** ₹0 (included in SaaS)
- **Referral Program:** ₹50/user
- **Weighted Average CAC:** ₹180

#### Hospital CAC
- **Sales Team Cost:** ₹50,000/hospital
- **Implementation & Onboarding:** ₹30,000
- **Total CAC:** ₹80,000/hospital

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

#### Patient LTV
- **Direct Premium:** ₹500/year × 3 years = ₹1,500
- **Insurance Commission:** ₹500 (one-time)
- **Financing Commission:** ₹1,000 (one-time)
- **Total LTV:** ₹3,000

**LTV:CAC Ratio = 3000:180 = 16.7:1** ✅ (Target > 3:1)

#### Hospital LTV
- **Annual SaaS:** ₹12L/year × 5 years = ₹60L
- **Churn Rate:** 10%/year
- **Total LTV:** ₹54L

**LTV:CAC Ratio = 5400000:80000 = 67.5:1** ✅

## Pricing Strategy

### Competitive Positioning
- **Hospital SaaS:** 30% cheaper than US equivalents (Zocdoc, HealthGrades)
- **Patient Features:** Free core features, premium for power users
- **Commission-based:** Zero upfront cost for insurers/NBFCs

### Price Optimization Tactics
1. **Annual Prepayment Discount:** 15% off for hospitals
2. **Volume Discounts:** Hospital chains get 20-30% off
3. **Bundling:** Package SaaS + advertising + API access
4. **Dynamic Pricing:** Adjust based on hospital size, location, specialty

### Upselling & Cross-selling
- **Basic → Professional:** Analytics insights, featured placement
- **Professional → Enterprise:** Custom integrations, white-label
- **Add-ons:** Premium support (₹10K/month), training (₹25K one-time)
